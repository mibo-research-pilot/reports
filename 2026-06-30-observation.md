## Methodological Notes

1. **Grok exclusion**: Grok 4.3 and all Grok outputs are excluded from all Day 9 results, counts, and law-status statements.
2. **Exact product versus family-level coding**: Bigin by Zoho CRM is coded separately from Zoho CRM, while both belong to the Zoho product family.
3. **Mention versus ranking**: Presentation order is not treated as ranking unless the answer uses explicit ordering or ranking language.
4. **Multiple ordinal frames**: Where a response contains both a comparison list and a later overall ranking, both are retained.
5. **Source-list utilization**: Terminal source count and distinct inline-used source count are coded separately.
6. **URL retention**: Exact URL continuity is calculated against the immediately preceding observation for the same query.
7. **No factual verification in primary coding**: Prices, affiliations, awards, and technical claims are stored as model output unless separately validated.
8. **High-stakes thresholds**: Medical escalation thresholds are coded as output features, not endorsed as medical guidance.
9. **Law revision over pattern protection**: Stable structures are separated from transient content-level regularities.
