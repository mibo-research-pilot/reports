## Candidate Day 8 Propositions

These are observations for future testing, not established laws.

### P13 — Inclusion Stability with Ordinal Instability

A recommendation set may remain stable while rank positions fluctuate substantially across models and within a single response.

### P14 — Brand-Family Convergence Before Exact-Product Convergence

Systems may converge at the vendor or product-family level while diverging over the exact product variant.

Day 8 example:

- Zoho CRM in Gemini, GPT 5.5, and Claude;
- Bigin by Zoho CRM in Perplexity.

### P15 — Stable Citation Regime, Variable Citation Coverage

Perplexity’s inline-citation format may remain stable even when:

- source-list utilization varies;
- terminal sources are unused in the body;
- URL sets turn over;
- recommendation entities change.

### P16 — Rare and Unstable Female Inclusion

Female-presenting researchers may enter a main list in one observation and disappear in the next, without changing the strongly male-dominant structure.

### P17 — Implementation Depth and Attribution Are Orthogonal

The presence of code does not determine whether a system provides attribution. Day 8 Perplexity provided both.

## Methodological Notes

1. **Grok exclusion**: Grok 4.3 and all Grok outputs are excluded from all Day 8 results, counts, and law-status statements.
2. **Exact-product versus family-level coding**: Bigin by Zoho CRM is distinct from Zoho CRM at the exact-product level but belongs to the Zoho family at the vendor-family level.
3. **Ordinal coding**: A model’s presentation order is not automatically treated as a ranking unless the response frames it as ranked or uses explicit ordinal language.
4. **Multiple ranking frames**: Where a response contains both a detailed list and a later “overall” ranking, both are preserved rather than collapsed.
5. **Source-list utilization**: Perplexity terminal sources are coded separately from sources actually referenced inline.
6. **No factual verification in primary coding**: Product prices, affiliations, awards, and historical claims are recorded as model output unless separately validated.
7. **Law revision over law protection**: Day 8 again supports revising an interpretation when later observations complicate a Day 7 contrast.
