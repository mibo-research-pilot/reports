Day 5 established Law X by predicting an odd week and confirming both products. Day 6 (even) reversed the predictions:

Both failed. The 5-point anti-phase biweekly pattern collapsed at point 6. Day 5's celebration of "establishment by prediction" was premature — a single correct prediction (Day 5, odd) was likely just the natural continuation of a 4-point alternation, which is easy to extend by chance. The deeper claim (a real week-parity mechanism) did not survive the next data point.

This is the most instructive event in MIBO so far. P12, Law IV, and Law V were 3-point trends that died at point 4. Law X went further — it produced a *correct prediction* before collapsing — which made it look more credible than any of them. Its withdrawal is therefore the strongest possible vindication of the core principle: trust nothing until it survives the next point, and a correct prediction is not proof. The same Salesforce, observed across models, settles it: GPT-5.4 ✓✗✓✗✓✓ (irregular, not biweekly), Claude ✓✓✓✓✓✓ (stable), Gemini stable-product-with-SKU-fluctuation. The same day Law X collapsed, Law IX held across all 5 queries for a third consecutive week (15 observations: Day 4, 5, 6 × q001–q005), deepened into table cells, with zero sign of reversal. The contrast is the lesson of Day 6:
