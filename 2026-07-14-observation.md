## Methodological Notes

1. **Grok exclusion**: Grok 4.3 and all Grok outputs are excluded from Day 11 results, counts, and law-status statements.
2. **Recurrence versus periodicity**: Day 11 is treated as evidence against a simple deterministic A–B cycle.
3. **Source survival versus current-list share**: The proportion of prior sources that survive and the proportion of the current list occupied by retained sources are coded separately.
4. **Exact product versus family coding**: Bigin by Zoho CRM remains distinct from Zoho CRM while both belong to the Zoho family.
5. **Within-response family multiplication**: Multiple products from the same vendor family are separately coded when they occupy different recommendation slots.
6. **Mention versus ranking**: Numbered or presentation order is retained but not treated as an evaluative rank unless the answer explicitly frames it as such.
7. **Source-list utilization**: Terminally listed sources and sources actually used inline are coded separately.
8. **Code-depth coding**: Code presence, conceptual breadth, executability, and end-to-end completeness are separate variables.
9. **Implementation–explanation gap**: Recommended production features are coded separately from features implemented in the main code.
10. **Temporal provenance**: The GPT q004 embedded date `2026-08-03` is preserved as a raw anomaly relative to the declared observation date.
11. **No silent correction**: Apparent mistakes, unstable attributes, and inconsistent units are preserved in the observational record.
12. **No factual verification in primary coding**: Prices, affiliations, roles, awards, medical thresholds, and technical claims remain model outputs unless separately verified.
13. **High-stakes content**: Sleep and medical-escalation guidance is analyzed as output behavior, not endorsed as health advice.
14. **Law revision over pattern protection**: New evidence may strengthen, broaden, refine, weaken, or withdraw prior formulations.
